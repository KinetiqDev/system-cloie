PROJECT CLOIE: System for **C**omprehensive **L**earning **O**utcomes **& I**nstructional **E**valuation **(CLOIE)**

## **1\. Purpose**

The CLOIES shall serve as a **department-level evaluation system** to assess the **attainment of Program Learning Outcomes (PLOs) and Graduate Outcomes (GOs)** using feedback from key stakeholders.

The system shall support **Outcome-Based Education (OBE)** principles by providing **evidence-based data** for program evaluation, quality assurance, and continuous improvement.

## **2\. Users**

The system shall be used by:

* Faculty Members  
* Students  
* Graduates / Alumni  
* Industry Partners / Employers  
* Department Heads / Deans

## **3\. Outcomes Management**

* The system shall allow authorized users to encode:  
  * Program Learning Outcomes (PLOs)  
  * Graduate Outcomes / Attributes (GOs)  
* Outcomes shall be linked to specific academic programs

## **4\. Evaluation Instruments**

* The system shall provide standardized evaluation tools aligned with OBE principles.  
* Evaluation instruments shall assess the **extent of outcome attainment** using:  
  * Defined rating scales (e.g., Attained, Partially Attained, Not Attained)  
  * Optional qualitative feedback

## **5\. Stakeholder-Based Evaluation**

* **Students** shall evaluate PLO attainment based on their course learning experience.  
* **Graduates** shall evaluate their achievement of graduate outcomes and program relevance.  
* **Industry partners** shall evaluate graduates or interns based on:  
  * Knowledge  
  * Skills  
  * Professional and ethical attributes

## **6\. Data Integrity and Confidentiality**

* The system shall ensure:  
  * Controlled evaluation periods  
  * One response per evaluator per cycle  
  * Confidential and anonymized data collection

## **7\. Reporting and Evidence Generation**

* The system shall generate consolidated reports showing:  
  * Level of attainment of PLOs and GOs  
  * Stakeholder-based comparative results  
* Reports shall serve as **documented evidence** for accreditation and program review.

## **8\. Review and Continuous Improvement**

* The system shall support documentation of:  
  * Outcome gaps  
  * Action items for program improvement  
* Historical data shall be retained for longitudinal analysis.

## **9\. System Boundaries**

The CLOIES shall **not**:

* Deliver instructional content  
* Assess individual student grades  
* Replace existing LMS or SIS platforms

## **10\. Expected Output**

The CLOIES shall provide the department with a **systematic, transparent, and auditable mechanism** to demonstrate:

* Program effectiveness  
* Graduate readiness  
* Alignment with Outcome-Based Education and accreditation standards

**Role of Sys CLOIE in the PDCA Cycle**

**PLAN**  
Sys CLOIE stores:

- Program Learning Outcomes  
- Graduate Outcomes  
- Outcome-aligned evaluation instruments

**DO**

- Teaching and learning activities occur \*\*outside\*\* Sys CLOIE.

**CHECK (Core Sys CLOIE Function)**

- Collects stakeholder evaluation data  
- Aggregates results by outcome  
- Determines level of outcome attainment  
- Produces evidence-based reports

**ACT**  
Department and college leadership:

- Review CLOIE System-generated reports  
- Identify gaps and strengths  
- Approve actions for improvement  
- Improvement actions are documented and linked to CLOIES results.
