### Recommended Use by Defense Stage

| Defense Stage | Primary Revision Focus | Usual Verifier |
| ----- | ----- | ----- |
| Title Defense | Problem/context, objectives, scope, proposed solution, initial RRL, and proposal/title refinements. | Capstone/Subject Adviser |
| Outline Defense | Required Chapters 1–4, methodology, requirements, use case/prototype, system design, and prototype/core-functionality revisions. | Capstone Adviser; designated panelist when necessary |
| Pre-Final Defense | Technical document, system completeness/functionality, testing, security/privacy, user-validation readiness, defects, and technical improvements. | Capstone Adviser; designated panelist for major conditions |
| Final Defense | Final manuscript corrections, system refinements, testing/validation evidence, documentation, results, and conditions for final clearance. | Capstone Adviser and/or Panel Chair/designated panelist |

**Implementation Note.** Use one fresh copy of this same form after each defense. The defense rubric records the evaluation and score; this form records only revisions that must be acted upon and verified. Minor typographical or formatting corrections may be consolidated into one entry.
