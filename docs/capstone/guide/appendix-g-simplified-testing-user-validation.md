### MINIMUM EVIDENCE TO RETAIN

Keep only enough supporting evidence to verify the activity. Appropriate evidence may include test results or logs, screenshots where useful, questionnaire or interview summaries, task/UAT checklists, issue records, relevant repository/build references, and signed user/client validation. Evidence may be stored digitally and referenced in the technical document rather than printing every artifact.

### SIMPLE TESTING AND VALIDATION FLOW

**TEST → RECORD FINDINGS → REVISE → RETEST → USER/STAKEHOLDER VALIDATE → SUMMARIZE → ADVISER REVIEW**

**Implementation Note.** Not every project requires every type of testing. The Adviser determines the testing and validation appropriate to the project’s requirements, technology, users, and risks. Projects involving sensitive data, security-critical functions, specialized hardware, AI, high transaction loads, or other higher-risk components may require additional technical test evidence beyond these simplified forms.
