# Chapter 2 — Review of Related Literature, Technologies and Systems

## What this chapter must demonstrate

Per the [official guide, Chapter 2](../guide/technical-document-guide-2026.md): build the evidence base for the project, establish what is already known or available, identify the gap or opportunity, and justify important requirements and technical choices. Sources must be organized by themes and synthesized (not one-source-at-a-time summaries); related systems must be compared with meaningful criteria; the synthesis must state the gap and the project's intended contribution. Prioritize peer-reviewed research, standards, official technical documentation, and credible professional sources — recent evidence (normally the last five years) with older seminal sources only when justified. Relevance and sufficiency govern; no arbitrary source count. Do not copy guide text; do not import claims from obsolete manuscript drafts.
