# General Education Institutional Learning Outcome evidence

Accepted on 2026-10-05 from the user's explicit decision to treat the General Education Coordinator as the Program Head of General Education Courses. This partially reverses the deferral recorded in ADR 0005 and in the Analytics context: **ILO evidence** is now in scope; **ILO attainment** and the **ILO-to-GO crosswalk** remain deferred.

ILO evidence is submitted Course-bound General Education ratings grouped through the typed CILO-to-ILO mappings, computed with the same rules as Program GO evidence: each rated question reaches a CILO through its frozen publication binding and then every ILO that CILO currently maps to; each submitted response item contributes once per `(response, evaluation, question, ILO)`; ratings are validated against the frozen instrument scale, invalid values are counted as excluded, means pool valid ratings at full precision with `spansMultipleScales` disclosure and per-scale distributions; manifestation (`LEARNING`, `PRACTICE`, `OPPORTUNITY`) is descriptive and never filters or weights. No direct question-to-ILO binding exists, so the CILO path is the only contribution path. Valid ratings that reach no ILO are reported as an unlinked count, split into general items and CILOs without an ILO mapping, so the evidence chain stays complete.

No ILO mapping is frozen at publication. Historical ratings therefore group by the current CILO-to-ILO mappings, and later mapping edits reinterpret history; the current-mapping disclosure applies and is forwarded into AI limitations. Archived ILOs keep their historical rows and are marked archived. A rating mean is never labelled attainment, achievement, or "met": no institutional targets exist.

The Coordinator analytics workspace adopts the Program Head tab model (`outcomes`, `courses`, `programs`, `trends`, `qualitative`); Programs replaces Stakeholders because Central Deployment evidence stays excluded from General Education analytics. The Coordinator responses workspace gains the Program Head review facets (status, response progress) plus class-context Program and ILO facets, and General Education review surfaces show ILO alignments instead of GO mappings. No schema migration is required.
