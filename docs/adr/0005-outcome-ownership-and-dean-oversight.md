# Outcome Ownership and Dean Oversight

## Status

Accepted

Amended 2026-08-14 for Issue #372: catalog-only amendment recording the Secretary-owned Institutional Learning Outcome catalog.

Amended 2026-08-15 for Issue #377: supersedes the #372 amendment's deferred claims. This amendment records the complete implemented Institutional Outcome layer — typed General Education CILO-to-Institutional Outcome mapping, Faculty mapping responsibility, Secretary stewardship and correction authority, Program Head read-only mapping review, Dean typed oversight, versioned immutable readiness snapshots, the new-publication alignment gate, and the irreversible legacy General Education cutover. ILO-to-GO crosswalks, dual CILO target mappings, and Insights/analytics over ILOs remain deferred and are not claimed here.

Amended 2026-08-20 for Issue #456: Program-specific readiness and the new-publication alignment gate now follow the exhaustive manifestation rule — every active CILO must carry a non-null manifestation (LEARNING, PRACTICE, or OPPORTUNITY) for every active PLO of the Course's owning Academic Program; a Program with zero active PLOs alongside active CILOs is incomplete, not ready. General Education keeps the at-least-one active Institutional Outcome rule. New readiness snapshots keep `schema_version = 2` and carry the exhaustive interpretation; version 1 snapshots and earlier version 2 snapshots keep their legacy at-least-one interpretation and are never rewritten.

Amended 2026-08-20: General Education CILO-to-ILO mappings now carry the same LEARNING, PRACTICE, or OPPORTUNITY manifestation as Program-specific CILO-to-PLO mappings. The Faculty alignment workspace uses the L/P/O matrix for both Course scopes. General Education readiness stays at-least-one: each active CILO needs at least one active Institutional Outcome mapping with a non-null manifestation. Legacy GE rows with a null manifestation are incomplete until Faculty classifies them. ILO-to-PLO crosswalks remain deferred.

## Context

System CLOIE recognizes three outcome layers: college-wide Institutional Learning Outcomes (ILOs), program-level Graduate Outcomes (GOs), and course-level CILOs. The college's revised policy uses two typed alignment paths: General Education CILOs map once at Course level to the shared Institutional Outcome catalog (applying to every assignment using that Course), and Program-specific CILOs map to Graduate Outcomes owned by their Course's owning Academic Program. The former General Education CILO-to-GO semantics were removed by the approved cutover.

Operational responsibility shifted in the same revision: Faculty is the primary mapper in the Course context, the Secretary owns the catalog and holds college-wide correction authority, Program Heads retain GO ownership but review mappings read-only, and the Dean has college-wide read-only oversight. The Wayfinder map "Dean dashboard information architecture" (Issue #103) accepted the role-owned-route rule that this model extends.

## Decision

1. **Institutional Outcomes ownership.** The Secretary owns the college-wide Institutional Learning Outcome catalog (`InstitutionalOutcome` / `institutional_outcomes`). Each Institutional Outcome has a stable unique code, statement stored as `description`, display `order`, active/archive state (`is_active`), and timestamps. Codes remain unique across archived records. Archive is soft state: archived outcomes remain visible for administrative and historical review but cannot be selected as future mapping targets. Restore returns an archived record to the active catalog in its stable display-order position. Dean, Program Head, and Faculty cannot mutate the catalog. Secretary catalog writes use exact before-and-after review, explicit confirmation, freshness recheck, and atomic persistence.
2. **Graduate Outcomes ownership.** Program Heads own their program Graduate Outcomes. They create, edit, reorder, archive, and restore GOs within their assigned program scope. Secretary has college-wide administrative authority to create, edit, reorder, and archive GOs, but does not become their accountable owner.
3. **CILO ownership.** Faculty author CILOs for the course contexts they teach, but the CILO itself belongs to the Course: CILOs are stored at the course level, stable across assignment periods, and never assignment-specific or faculty-owned. Secretary has college-wide administrative authority to create, edit, and archive CILOs, but does not become their accountable owner.
4. **Typed mapping relations.** Two typed relations are the only mapping paths. `cilo_institutional_outcome_mappings` maps General Education CILOs to Institutional Outcomes; `cilo_mappings` maps Program-specific CILOs to Graduate Outcomes of the Course's owning Academic Program. Both relations enforce unique CILO-target pairs, foreign keys, lookup indexes, and provenance (creator/updater and timestamps for new or changed writes; legacy rows with no actor remain explicitly unattributed). Database scope backstops reject wrong-layer writes: General Education CILOs map only to active Institutional Outcomes, and Program-specific CILOs map only to their owning Program's GOs. New mapping targets must be active.
5. **Faculty mapping responsibility.** Faculty is the primary operational mapper for both typed relations through the Course alignment workspace. Faculty authorization requires an active Course Assignment owned by the current Faculty member in an active Academic Period; affiliation alone is insufficient. General Education alignment is Course-level and shared: one CILO-to-ILO mapping set applies to every assignment using that Course, and the surface warns that a change affects the shared Course. Program-specific alignment offers only the owning Program's active GOs. Faculty stages a complete Course-level addition/removal diff, reviews exact before/after changes, and commits atomically with a freshness recheck; no immediate per-selection writes occur.
6. **Secretary stewardship.** The Secretary owns the Institutional Outcome catalog and has college-wide correction authority for both typed mapping relations, using the protected-write contract: exact before-and-after review, explicit confirmation, server-side authority, freshness recheck, and atomic save with provenance. Secretary does not become accountable owner of GOs or CILOs.
7. **Program Head read-only review.** Program Heads retain Graduate Outcome ownership (create, edit, reorder, archive, restore) within assigned Program scope, but mapping review is read-only: they inspect valid typed mappings and readiness gaps for their Program and cannot create or remove mapping rows through the UI or crafted server requests.
8. **Dean oversight.** The Dean has college-wide **read-only**, period-scoped, privacy-safe oversight of the Institutional Outcome catalog, Program-specific GO coverage, and typed mapping gaps. General Education gaps are labeled as Institutional Outcome gaps, never as missing Program GOs. Reads return `private, no-store` and expose no student identifiers, roster data, responses, or private account metadata. The Dean does not edit outcomes or mappings.
9. **Readiness and publication.** Readiness is evaluated per unique `(Course, Academic Program)` context from active Course Assignments: `missing-cilos` when no active CILOs exist, `incomplete-mapping` when any active CILO fails the typed alignment rule, and `ready` only when every active CILO satisfies it. General Education CILOs require at least one active Institutional Outcome mapping (at-least-one rule). Program-specific CILOs require a non-null manifestation (LEARNING, PRACTICE, or OPPORTUNITY) for every active PLO owned by the Course's owning Academic Program (exhaustive rule); a Program with zero active PLOs alongside active CILOs is incomplete, not vacuously ready. Archived targets, wrong-program rows, and mapping rows without a manifestation never satisfy live readiness or new publication. New completed-period snapshots carry `schema_version = 2` with the exhaustive typed interpretation and are immutable; existing snapshots — version 1 and earlier version 2 — retain their legacy at-least-one interpretation and are never rewritten by later mapping or catalog changes. New Course-bound evaluation publication is rejected while any active CILO lacks a valid active target, with a direct repair path to Course alignment.
10. **Legacy cutover.** The irreversible migration deleted exactly the legacy General Education CILO-to-GO rows — predicate: CILO belongs to a Course with `course_scope = 'GENERAL_EDUCATION'` — after an operator-reviewed preflight count and dry run. Program-specific CILO-to-GO rows, CILOs, GOs, published evaluations, and completed readiness snapshots were preserved. Historical PLO-era migrations remain historical and were not edited or revived.
11. **Deferred.** ILO-to-GO crosswalks, mapping the same CILO to both ILOs and GOs, per-Program or per-assignment General Education mapping rows, and Insights/analytics/attainment based on ILOs are explicitly deferred. No automatic semantic conversion of legacy General Education rows was attempted.
12. **Learning Outcomes vs. Insights.** Learning Outcomes holds setup and alignment: Institutional Outcomes, GOs, CILOs, and both typed mapping relations. A future Insights area, currently hidden in navigation, will hold response-based Learning Evaluation Results, Analytics, and Reports.
13. **Retired labels.** `CILO Reviews` is not a standalone Dean information-architecture label. It does not appear in current Dean navigation or group landing pages.

## Consequences

- The role-owned-route rule from the Course Catalog and Assignments context still applies: even where Secretary and Dean share operational rules for course-assignment stewardship, their dashboards remain separate role-owned routes. The same separation applies to Learning Outcomes: the Dean view is read-only typed oversight, the Secretary view owns Institutional Outcome catalog administration and correction, the Program Head view is GO authoring plus read-only mapping review, and the Faculty view is CILO authoring plus Course alignment.
- This amendment supersedes the #372 amendment wherever the two conflict (notably its statements that typed General Education mapping was deferred and that the Dean surface did not present ILO-mapping coverage). Implementation tickets (#371–#377), the prototype (Issue #105), and the Wayfinder map (Issue #103) can cite this ADR as the authoritative ownership rule set.
- The Dean does not acquire edit capability for outcomes or mappings. If a future effort changes this, it must amend this ADR explicitly.
- Mapping integrity is enforced at both service and database layers: unique CILO-target pairs, scope backstops, Program-scope validation, and active-target requirements for both typed relations. Live readiness reports incomplete mappings without blocking step-by-step CILO or GO authoring; the alignment gate applies to new Course-bound evaluation publication only.
- Legacy General Education CILO-to-GO rows were deleted by the approved irreversible migration. New and changed mapping writes carry actor and timestamp provenance; legacy rows that predate provenance remain unattributed and the system does not fabricate actors.
- The outdated `docs/cloie-prd.md` and `docs/cloie-srs.md` are not authoritative for the rules in this ADR; the Wayfinder map (Issue #103), this ADR, and the OpenSpec change `introduce-institutional-learning-outcomes` are.
